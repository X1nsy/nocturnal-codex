00 / Start here

You are meant to die. The Codex is what you keep.

Hades II is a progression game wearing a roguelike's mask. Every failed night should buy something permanent: a card, a tool, a keepsake, a shortcut. Do the five things below, then use the other tabs when you need the exact recipe.

Before the door

The first night, in order.

Do this between runs, not mid-fight. The goal is to make the next night different from the last one.

Five moves before you launch

The order matters. Spending first turns a death into progress before you even enter Erebus.

  1. 01
    Take the Witch's Staff

    It is the tutorial weapon for a reason: safe range, clear Omega attacks, and the least punishing Magick loop. Learn one weapon before you learn five.

  2. 02
    Spend Ash, then Psyche

    Ash unlocks cards; Psyche raises Grasp. If you have exactly 10 Grasp, equip the five-card set below before you touch anything else.

  3. 03
    Equip one Keepsake

    Luckier Tooth is the blind pick. Without it, take a god's keepsake to force the core Boon your build needs.

  4. 04
    Give one Nectar

    The first gift to an eligible character returns their Keepsake. That is a permanent option, not a one-night bonus.

  5. 05
    Read the Cauldron

    After every return, check it before you launch again. The first three Incantations cost one Moly each and change how the run plays.

The long arc

The first few nights, not the first perfect run.

Each phase has one job. Clear that job, then spend. Death is the schedule.

Nights 1–3Learn

Build the habit

Stay on the Staff. Learn the Altar, the Cauldron, and the difference between a Boon door and a resource door.

  • Buy the three one-Moly Incantations.
  • Reach Hecate with a plan, not a full build.
  • Spend everything on return.
Nights 4–6Widen

Open the toolbox

Unlock Sister Blades and Umbral Flames. Buy Woodsy Lifespring, then start the familiar chain.

  • Gift Nectar to every new character once.
  • Use Forget-Me-Not to target recipes.
  • Try one new weapon, not all of them.
Nights 7+Open

Raise the ceiling

Unlock Aspects, Kindred Keepsakes, and the surface. Take the Oath only after your first Chronos clear.

  • Spend Nightmare on one Aspect, not five.
  • Swap keepsakes at region breaks.
  • Finish Fateful Bond before farming surface.

Combat

Six rules that save runs.

None of these require a specific god or weapon. They are the floor, not the ceiling.

Cast is a trap

Drop it first. Enemies inside are held and take more damage; Omega moves land easier.

Dash late

Dash has a brief invulnerability window. Save it for the attack, not the approach.

Break yellow first

Yellow health bars are armored. Normal hits cannot stagger until the armor is gone.

Omega is a commitment

Charging leaves you exposed. Do not channel into an incoming swing or a projectile volley.

Magick is per room

It refills between locations. Spend it during the fight; do not save it for the boss door.

Two gods, one plan

Pick two cores and aim for their Duo. A third god is support, not a new build.

The last two levers

Carry the run. Skip the noise.

Your Keepsake and your patience do more for a beginner than another damage roll.

Keepsakes that carry

Luckier Tooth is the blind pick. A god keepsake forces the core Boon you need. Frinos adds Life and blocks projectiles; Toula adds a Death Defiance.

02 / Forge the pair

Thirty-seven Duos, and the exact doors they open.

A Duo is not a random reward. It is a recipe: one core Boon from each parent, both slots protected, and a pool that has not been diluted by a third god. Every card below lists the exact keys.

37duos · 9 gods

The forge recipe

Four steps from two gods to one Duo

36 god pairs · 37 recipes

Duos are offered by either parent once both core Boons are owned. The hard part is not the fight; it is keeping the pool narrow enough for the offer to surface.

01

Seed the pair

Take the keepsake of one parent in Erebus, the other in Oceanus. Two gods in the pool, no third until the Duo lands.

02

Own the exact core

A “core” is the Strike, Flourish, Ring, Rush, or Gain named on the card. A sibling Boon from the same god does not count.

03

Protect the slot

Do not sell a qualifying core for a shiny Epic. If a god offers a Duo, clear the slot or take it; the next roll may not repeat.

04

Take the Rare floor

Duo offers use a Rare floor. Queen and Divinity improve the odds of seeing one; they do not replace the prerequisite.

Diagnostics

Duo not showing?

Six reasons the recipe failed, in the order they usually happen.

One parent is missing

Both gods must be in the pool and both cores owned. A Duo from a god who has not spoken yet cannot roll.

The core is a cousin

Flame Strike is not Flame Flourish. Check the exact names under each parent; only those keys open the door.

A third god diluted the pool

Every extra god adds offers. Once the two cores are owned, refuse new gods until the Duo appears.

You declined the offer

Duos can be skipped. If you took the other slot, the pair may not return this region. Protect the next one.

Queen versus King

Queen's Ransom and King's Ransom are mutually exclusive. Taking one removes the other for the night.

A qualifying Boon was sold

Purging a core closes the recipe. If you need the Gold, re-acquire the exact core before fishing again.
